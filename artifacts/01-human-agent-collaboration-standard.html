Agent UX operating standard

Human-Agent Collaboration Standard

Autonomy is earned.

The product standard is calibrated agency: the right amount of machine autonomy for the task, risk, user, and moment.

Standard Statement

Great agent products make humans more capable, not less informed. Plans should be visible, actions inspectable, assumptions editable, memory governable, autonomy scoped, and recovery possible.

Collaboration Loop

Define Human sets goal, constraints, success criteria, and risk.
Plan Agent proposes steps, assumptions, risks, and gates.
Approve / Adjust Human edits, blocks, delegates, or narrows scope.
Act Agent executes inside agreed boundaries.
Report Agent shows outcomes, evidence, changes, and unknowns.
Intervene Human pauses, redirects, takes over, or rolls back.
Learn Corrections become approved preferences or rules.

Autonomy Ladder

L0

Suggest onlyRecommend actions, but do not draft or execute.

L1

Draft and waitCreate proposed work, then stop for approval.

L2

Execute reversible actionsPerform low-risk work with clear undo paths.

L3

Execute bounded workflowsComplete scoped work with approval gates.

L4

Manage a domainMonitor, prioritize, and act with periodic review.

Trust Requirements

Visible PlanShow intent before consequential action.
Editable AssumptionsSeparate known facts from guesses.
Evidence TrailsConnect claims to sources and tool outputs.
Inspectable ActionsLog tool calls, outputs, and decisions.
Governable MemoryExpose what is remembered and why.
Recovery PathPreview, undo, or show manual repair.
